## Project Overview

This research focuses on the design and optimization of longitudinal control systems for autonomous vehicles (AVs). The core of the study is the application of **Fuzzy Logic Controllers (FLC)** and their integration with traditional **PID controllers** to create advanced, hybrid control systems.

The primary goal is to enhance the safety, reliability, and efficiency of autonomous vehicles. By developing controllers capable of managing the dynamic and complex nature of real-world driving environments, this project aims to improve the vehicle's response time and stability.

![autonomous vehicle](av.png)

## Key Objectives & Methods

- **Fuzzy Logic Control**: Explores the use of fuzzy logic to handle the ambiguities and uncertainties inherent in longitudinal vehicle control (e.g., acceleration, braking, and maintaining safe distances).
- **Hybrid FLC-PID System**: A significant part of the research involves designing and testing a hybrid system that combines the adaptive strengths of fuzzy logic with the stability of PID control.
- **Prototype Development**: The project includes the design of a prototype model of an autonomous vehicle to serve as a testbed for the developed control algorithms.
- **Performance Analysis**: Implementation and testing of the control systems demonstrated significant improvements in system response and stability, validating the potential for real-world application.
