We are thrilled to share that Khaled and his team were selected as **finalists** at **Youth Tech Begin 2025**, a flagship entrepreneurship event organized under the Karınca GO Acceleration Program at **Medeniyet Teknopark**, Istanbul.

Their project introduces a **next-generation point-of-care diagnostic biosensor**, designed to detect multiple biomarkers from a single drop of blood. Combining **engineering design**, **biomedical insight**, and **computational intelligence**, the system offers compact and rapid health diagnostics—tailored for real-world impact.

> “Being part of this journey alongside such a passionate team has been an honor. We’re excited about what lies ahead.” — Khaled

Special thanks to the organizers, mentors, and jury members for cultivating a platform that celebrates innovation and global collaboration among youth.
