# Honored by Karabuk University Rector

On 30 May 2024, at the Hamit Çepni Conference Hall, Khaled was presented with a certificate of appreciation by Karabuk University’s Rector, Prof. Dr. Fatih KIRIŞIK.
The occasion honored students and advisors whose projects were accepted in the 2023 second-term TÜBİTAK 2209 A-B program, a highly competitive initiative coordinated by the Technology Transfer Office to spotlight the university’s leading research and innovation efforts.

Khaled’s project, selected under the TÜBİTAK 2209A scheme, underscores his exceptional research acumen and the potential impact of his work. The recognition also reflects the university’s commitment to fostering talent and advancing scientific knowledge. Khaled expressed gratitude to the administration, faculty, and fellow researchers for their unwavering support and affirmed his resolve to contribute further to cutting-edge inquiry.
