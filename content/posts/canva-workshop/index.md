We are proud to announce the successful conclusion of our "Canva Basics – Level 1" training, held on April 5-6-7, 2025. This workshop was one of the most inspiring events of the year, marked by incredible engagement and a shared passion for learning over more than four continuous hours.

In recognition of their commitment, 112 certificates of attendance were awarded to participants who met the 70% attendance requirement.

The training culminated in a vibrant design competition that showcased the incredible talent of our community. With 43 participants and 15 selected designs, the competition highlighted a remarkable diversity of skill and creativity.

A special thank you to the Tamayuz Platform for University Services and to the inspiring instructor, Eng. Khaled Hamidi, for his invaluable contributions.

We promise that the best is yet to come. Stay tuned for our next series: "A Research Methodology with a Digital Mind: Academic and Technical Skills for a New Era."
