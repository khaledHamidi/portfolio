The Robotech Team, in partnership with the Baytna organization, is pleased to announce a call for trainers and facilitators for its new project, **"Your Peace is My Space."**

We are seeking qualified individuals to lead training sessions in the following areas:
1.  **The Concept of Peace-Building**
2.  **Project Proposal Writing for Peace-Building**
3.  **Digital Citizenship**
4.  **Digital Safety**

Additionally, we are looking for a facilitator for community dialogue sessions.
