We are thrilled to announce the launch of the first event for our long-awaited project, **"Your Peace is My Space,"** developed to meet current community needs. It is wonderful to see our vision become a reality, planting seeds of hope and providing tools for change both on the ground and in the digital space.

In partnership with the Baytna organization, Robotech Team kicked off the project with a special training session on the **"Concept of Peace-Building."** The workshop saw active participation from youth and women's groups, as well as individuals with disabilities, in the city of Azaz.

The event was enriched by the participation of 16 civil society organizations and volunteer teams, which broadened the scope of discussions and collaboration.

We believe that peace-building begins with a deep understanding of its core concepts, empowering individuals to become agents of change in their communities, both physically and digitally.
