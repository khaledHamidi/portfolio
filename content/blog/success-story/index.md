**In June 2025 Dr. Musab Al-Shebib — a Syrian researcher and academic — chose engineer *Khaled Hamidi* to inaugurate his new “Success Stories” series.**  
The recorded conversation traces Khaled’s path from shattered classrooms in Syria, through the hurdles of life as a refugee in Türkiye, to graduating **first** in Mechatronics Engineering at Karabuk University’s Faculty of Technology.

Key moments covered in the interview:

- Growing up amid conflict and studying under the roar of war-planes rather than school bells.  
- Crossing into Türkiye with little more than determination — and hundreds of unanswered questions.  
- Overcoming the language barrier, mastering Turkish, and turning exile into motivation rather than limitation.  
- Achieving **rank #1** in Karabuk University’s Faculty of Technology (Mechatronics).  

> “Khaled refused to be just a statistic on a refugee list; instead, he became *number one* on the list of excellence.” — Dr. Musab Al-Shebib

### Turkish Summary | Bir Başarı Hikayesi

Zorlukların arttığı bir dönemde, Halid Hamidi’nin hikâyesi en ilham verici örneklerden biri oldu.  
Suriye’de savaş ortamında eğitimine başlayan Halid, Türkiye’ye sığınmacı olarak geldi.  
Türkçeyi kısa sürede öğrenerek *Karabük Üniversitesi Mekatronik Mühendisliği* bölümünde **birinci** olmayı başardı.  
Bu sadece bir diploma değil; azmin ve iradenin zaferidir.
